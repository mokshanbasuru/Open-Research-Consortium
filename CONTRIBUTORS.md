# Contributors

The Open Research Consortium is the work of its contributors. The authoritative record of who contributed what is the Git history of this repository, which can be listed with:

```sh
git shortlog --summary --numbered --email
```

Every contribution is attributed to its author through the `Signed-off-by` trailer required by the [Developer Certificate of Origin](CONTRIBUTING.md#developer-certificate-of-origin).

This file recognises contributors whose work has been substantial or whose role warrants specific acknowledgement. It is edited by hand, and entries are added by Maintainers on request or on their own initiative. Recognition here is an acknowledgement and does not confer governance rights. Governance roles are defined in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) and recorded in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md).
