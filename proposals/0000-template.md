# CP-0000: Short, descriptive title

## Summary

One paragraph describing the proposal.

## Motivation

Describe the problem this proposal addresses and why it is worth solving. Include relevant background and, where applicable, evidence of the problem.

## Proposal

Describe the change in enough detail that it can be evaluated and implemented. State precisely which documents or processes are affected and how.

## Rationale and Alternatives

Explain why this approach was chosen. Describe the alternatives considered, including taking no action, and why they were not preferred.

## Impact

Describe the effects on Contributors, Maintainers, existing content, licensing, and the Project's independence. Identify any risks and how they are mitigated.

## Transition and Implementation

Describe how and when the change will take effect, and who is responsible for implementing it.

## Unresolved Questions

List open questions that remain to be settled during discussion.

## Decision Record

To be completed when the proposal is decided: the decision level and rule applied, the votes cast, and a summary of the principal objections and how they were addressed.
